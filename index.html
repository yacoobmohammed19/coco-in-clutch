The Maison

Small enough to hold.
Impossible to overlook.

Coco in Clutch began with a simple belief: the smallest piece you carry should be the one people remember.

We curate evening clutches for South African celebrations, from crystal-covered minaudières to soft pleated satin, choosing each design for its silhouette, its sparkle and the way it completes an outfit.

Every clutch arrives gift-wrapped and ready for its first night out.
